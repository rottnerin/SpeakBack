SpeakBack: how it works

An oral practice tool for IB Spanish Ab Initio. Students record themselves describing a photo and get rubric-based feedback in a couple of minutes.

What the student does

RecordSpeak for about 3–5 minutes, alone, describing a photo. No examiner, no conversation.
UploadAdd their name, the audio file, and (optionally) the photo they described.
WaitWhile the AI works, a falling-balloon vocabulary game keeps them busy.
Read feedbackMarks plus quoted evidence from their own speech. They can save it as a PDF.

What happens behind the scenes

  1. Photo reading (if a photo was sent): the AI notes what is actually in the image, privately.
  2. Transcription: the recording is turned into verbatim Spanish. If there is no real student speech (under 30 words), the app stops and asks the student to re-record. Nothing is graded or saved.
  3. Two independent graders: one listens to the audio (pronunciation, fluency, delivery), the other reads only the transcript (grammar, vocabulary, content). Each scores evidence-first.
  4. A judge reconciles them: trusts the audio grader for Criterion A, the transcript grader for B1, checks claims against the photo, and writes the student feedback.

What is graded

Scored
  • Criterion A, Language: /12
  • Criterion B1, Photo description: /6
  • Subtotal: /18
Not scored
  • B2 (Conversation) and C (Interaction) are never assessed or penalised.
  • No /30 total and no IB grade 1–7 (the grade table is built for the full exam and would understate students).
Rules from your commentary
  • Every claim must be backed by a quote.
  • B1 is capped at 4/6 without a developed cultural connection.
  • With no photo, B1 judges structure and language only.
  • Spoken scores on sample recordings are ignored.

Who sees what

Student
  • English feedback with marks only (A, B1, subtotal), never bands.
  • Their own Spanish quoted back, with grammar terms in Spanish.
  • Targets as marks, e.g. "B1 3/6 → 5/6".
  • Never sees the transcript.
You, at /admin (password-protected)
  • Every submission: transcript, feedback and scores.
  • Where you check whether the AI agrees with your own marking.
  • Internally the AI reasons in bands to reach marks; those stay on your side.

Privacy

Audio and photos are never stored. They exist in memory only while the request runs, then are gone. Photos may show people. Only the transcript, feedback and scores are saved to the database. Recordings and photos are sent to Google's Gemini for processing.

How you tune it

The whole grading logic lives in one plain-text file, feedback.md: the descriptors, the scoring rules and the cultural cap. It is read fresh on every submission, so an edit takes effect immediately with no redeploy. Any change to the rubric is worth discussing first, since the rubric is the product.

Still to validate The A and B1 descriptors come from 55 full-exam transcripts, but the monologue-specific level differences and the photo-accuracy markers are new. Please compare the AI's marks with yours on a handful of real photo-description recordings before relying on them.
