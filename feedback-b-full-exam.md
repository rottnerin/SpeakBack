## 1. Full Rubric — All Criteria

### Criterion A: Language (12)
| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Command of the language is limited. Vocabulary is sometimes appropriate to the task. Basic grammatical structures are used. Language contains errors in basic structures that interfere with communication. Pronunciation and intonation are influenced by other language(s); mispronunciations are recurrent and interfere with communication. |
| 4–6 | Command of the language is partially effective. Vocabulary is appropriate to the task. Some basic grammatical structures are used, with some attempts at more complex structures. Language is mostly accurate in basic structures, but errors occur in more complex structures and at times interfere with communication. Pronunciation/intonation are influenced by other language(s) but mispronunciations do not often interfere. |
| 7–9 | Command of the language is effective and mostly accurate. Vocabulary is appropriate and varied. A variety of basic and more complex grammatical structures is used. Occasional errors in basic and complex structures do not interfere with communication. Pronunciation and intonation are easy to understand. |
| 10–12 | Command of the language is mostly accurate and very effective. Vocabulary is appropriate, varied, and includes idiomatic expressions. A variety of basic and more complex grammatical structures is used effectively. Minor errors in more complex structures do not interfere with communication. Pronunciation and intonation are easy to understand and help convey meaning. |

### Criterion B1: Message — Visual Stimulus (6)
| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | The presentation is mostly irrelevant to the stimulus, limited to incomplete descriptions, and not clearly linked to the target culture(s). |
| 3–4 | The presentation is mostly relevant; with a focus on explicit details, the candidate provides descriptions and basic personal interpretations; mostly linked to the target culture(s). |
| 5–6 | The presentation is consistently relevant, drawing on explicit and implicit details, with descriptions and personal interpretations, and makes clear links to the target culture(s). |

### Criterion B2: Message — Conversation (6) — NOT assessed by SpeakBack (no conversation recorded)
| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Candidate consistently struggles to address the questions; some responses appropriate, rarely developed; limited scope and depth. |
| 3–4 | Responses mostly relevant to the questions; most appropriate, some developed; mostly broad in scope and depth. |
| 5–6 | Responses consistently relevant and show development; consistently appropriate and developed; broad in scope and depth, including personal interpretations and/or attempts to engage the interlocutor. |

### Criterion C: Interactive Skills — Communication (6) — NOT assessed by SpeakBack (no interaction recorded)
| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Comprehension and interaction limited. Limited responses in the target language. Participation limited; most questions must be repeated/rephrased. |
| 3–4 | Comprehension and interaction mostly sustained. Candidate responds in the target language and mostly demonstrates comprehension. Participation mostly sustained. |
| 5–6 | Comprehension and interaction consistently sustained. Candidate responds in the target language and demonstrates comprehension. Participation sustained with some independent contributions. |

## 2. Checklist (teacher's own, Spanish)

**Checklist LANGUAGE / Tiempos verbales:** Presente, Reflexivos, Acciones: Pretérito indefinido,
Descripciones: Pretérito imperfecto, Pretérito perfecto, Condicional, Futuro, Presente subjuntivo,
Pretérito imperfecto subjuntivo. Dudo que / tal vez / ojalá que / no creo que / no me parece que +
SUBJUNTIVO. Verbos complejos. Conectores de oro. Verbos tipo "me gusta"; "me da rabia/pena", "hace
falta que", "me alegré de que" + subjuntivo. Reflexivos, Pronombre OD-OI. WEIRDO + Subjuntivo. Aunque,
cuando + Subjuntivo. Condicional 1, 2, 3. Subordinadas: que, el cual, cuyo.

**Checklist Criterion B1 — Visual Stimulus:** Descripción – interpretación + experto. Contenido
cultural — "esto es lo más importante." Desarrollo, explicación del contenido cultural,
interpretación. Conexión entre la foto, el tema y la cultura. La foto como pretexto para hablar de
cultura; experto en el tema.

**Checklist Criterion B2 — Conversation (not used):** Prepara las ideas, conectores, argumentos,
frases para interactuar, expresiones de acuerdo y desacuerdo, expresiones de opinión.

**Checklist Criterion C — Communication (not used):** Explicas → desarrollas → das ejemplos. Opinas,
comparas, deduces. Hay detalles y ejemplos. Muchos conectores: aunque, después de, para concluir, no
obstante, sin embargo, por lo tanto, desafortunadamente, de hecho, puesto que, debido a, ya que, por
consiguiente.

## 3. Candidate Benchmarks by Grade Band (full oral, /30 — includes B2 and C)

Real anonymized candidates, awarded marks, and teacher comments, grouped by grade band. Useful for a
future full-exam mode; the A/B1-only evidence from these was pulled into `feedback-spanish-b.md`'s
performance-level differentiators.

**Grade Band 7 (27–28/30):** Exceptional accuracy and varied vocabulary; advanced tenses (subjunctive,
conditional) with minimal slips; complete B1 presentations with explicit/implicit detail and rich,
unprompted cultural elaboration; B2/C fully developed and fluently sustained with zero hesitation.

**Grade Band 6 (25/30):** Good grammar and appropriate topic vocabulary; mostly consistent tense
control with occasional hesitation; B1 presentations relevant with clear cultural connection, though
sometimes not extended beyond the obvious; B2/C solid, developed, sustained with independent
contributions.

**Grade Band 5 (19–23/30):** Functional-to-good language, typically limited by missing subjunctive or
other complex-structure gaps; B1 ranges from strong (cultural link well evidenced) to a cap case
(one candidate scored 1/6 on B1 for entirely omitting the cultural-connection segment despite strong
A, B2 and C); B2/C generally solid with some reliance on teacher prompts.

**Grade Band 4 (14–18/30):** Partially effective language with basic structures and a noticeable
error rate, often missing subjunctive or tense variety; B1 relevant but with mostly basic
interpretation and direct (not deeply developed) cultural references; B2/C brief, limited in depth,
reliant on teacher scaffolding.

**Grade Band 2–3 (12/30):** Persistent grammatical breakdowns and severe vocabulary deficits; B1 can
fail the stated structure entirely (one candidate's presentation ran under a minute and omitted the
target-culture link); B2/C marked by long silences and inability to sustain or develop responses.
